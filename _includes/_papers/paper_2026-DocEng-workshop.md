

### <a href="{{site.baseurl}}/publications/2026-DocEng-workshop-slides.pdf">Accessible Presentations with LaTeX</a>

+ Slides for the workshop on accessible presentations at DocEng 2026
+ Keywords: LaTeX, tagging, accessibility, presentations

This set of slides covers the key topics discussed at a half-day workshop at
DocEng 2026 focussing on creating accessible presentations. The first covers the
more general challenges of accessible PDF production, before moving to specific
requirements for presentations. The source for the live demonstrations used in
the session form part of the file, but are also available at
https://texdev.net/ltx-talk/examples.
