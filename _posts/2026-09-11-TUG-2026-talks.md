# Talks at TUG 2026 (from the team and others)

Frank, Ulrike and Marcel attended the [TUG 2026](https://tug.org/tug2026/)
meeting in early August in Calgary. They presented two papers there

- Ulrike gave an update on the Tagged PDF project
  ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fOumWbb1amQ) and <a
  href="{{site.baseurl}}/publications/2026-UFi-TUG-tb146fischer-tagging26.pdf">paper</a>),
  showing off how things are working and giving advice on keeping up to date
  with developments generally in this work. For most end users, the tagging
  project is quite transparent (minimal source changes), so this is an
  opportunity to see 'under the hood' a bit.
- Frank talked about updating document classes using templates
  ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SrCCaSrtVkE) and <a
  href="{{site.baseurl}}/publications/2026-FMi-TUG-tb146mittelbach-accessibleclasses.pdf">paper</a>).
  This is more aimed at document class authors, who will need to think about
  taking steps to update their code to let users have that transparent
  experience.

But it wasn't only the team who talked about tagged PDFs and accessibility.
Timothy Erickson and Aarush Bhat  
talked about the realties of making content accessible for a visually impaired
users ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k0VdJCfZiso)): Aarush is blind.
This gave a real sense of the day-to-day requirements for truly accessible
content. Tim Prescott gave _two_ talks, back to back, one focussed on converting
a textbook to tagged PDF ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U0OzGV7qkfA)
and [paper](https://tug.org/tug2026/preprints/prescott-book-accessibility.pdf))
and a second on making accessible presentations using `ltx-talk`
([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pltqpUBeG24) and
[paper](https://tug.org/tug2026/preprints/prescott-ltx-talk.pdf)). Tim has been
very active in working with the team to deliver for US education, so it was
excellent to hear him. Finally, John Lienhard looked at his work on the MIT
thesis class ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gTtfFAD7sLk) and
[paper](https://tug.org/tug2026/preprints/lienhard-mitthesis.pdf)), making use
of the new template methods to update the flexibility of the class as well as
producing accessible results.

Overall, the conference was an excellent showcase of the work in the TeX community on
building accessibility into standard workflows.

Enjoy --- Joseph
