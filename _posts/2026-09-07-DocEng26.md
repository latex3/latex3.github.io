# Accessible presentations - workshop at DocEng'26 in Fribourg

In late August, Ulrike, Frank, and I attended DocEng'26 Conference in Fribourg,
Switzerland. We ran a half-day workshop on creating accessible presentations. We
started with the core ideas around accessibility and how they impact on PDFs,
before moving to look at the particular challenges presented by presentations.
We finished the sessions with discussions with the attendees about various open
challenges, for example whether to represent frame structure in the tag structure.

The [slides](../../publications/2026-DocEng-workshop-slides.pdf) and
[examples](https://texdev.net/ltx-talk/examples) are available online; most of
the presentation-specific material was shows as 'live demos'.
